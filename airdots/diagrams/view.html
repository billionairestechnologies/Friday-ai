Discussion

sequenceDiagram
  participant User
  participant Friday
  User->>Friday: Speaks
  Friday->>User: Answers
  Note over Friday: Waits. Does not talk again.
  User->>Friday: Speaks again
  Friday->>User: Answers or asks one question
  Note over Friday: Waits for the answer.

Hold or release

flowchart TD
  turn[User turn]
  estimate[Estimate duration]
  hold[Hold the call]
  speak[Speak the result]
  release[Speak the plan and release the call]
  job[Job keeps running]
  deliver[Deliver on the chosen channel]
  overrun[Hold ran too long]
  turn --> estimate
  estimate -->|"about 5 to 10 seconds"| hold
  hold --> speak
  hold --> overrun
  overrun --> release
  estimate -->|"longer than that"| release
  release --> job --> deliver
  speak --> deliver

Making a job

sequenceDiagram
  participant User
  participant Friday
  participant Runtime
  participant Notifier
  User->>Friday: States the work and the requirements
  Friday->>Runtime: Classify kind and duration
  alt Needs a yes
    Friday->>User: Repeats the plan
    User->>Friday: Yes
  end
  alt Short job
    Friday->>User: Getting the details
    Runtime-->>Friday: Result
    Friday->>User: Speaks the result
  else Long job
    Friday->>User: Started. Names the channel. Call ends.
    Runtime-->>Notifier: Ready, with link
    Notifier-->>User: App, Telegram, WhatsApp, email, or a short callback
  end
