# Hold or release

The runtime estimates the job before it burns the line. About ₹4 per connected minute.

## Short

Use this when the work should finish in about 5–10 seconds.

1. Friday says she is getting the details.
2. The tool, skill, or status read runs.
3. She speaks the result.
4. She asks, only if they have not already said, whether they also want it on the app, Telegram, WhatsApp, or email.
5. The call stays open. She waits.

If the work passes `phone.max_held_seconds`, she says she is continuing it off the line. The job flips to long. Delivery follows their choice or their settings.

## Long

Use this for deep research, build mode, a sandbox run, an agent loop, or any workflow that has to execute.

1. Friday states the plan in one or two sentences when the job needs a yes.
2. On yes, she says she has started it and names the channel.
3. The call ends.
4. The job keeps running.
5. The notifier delivers progress and the link. A callback happens only if they opted in.

"Stay with me" keeps the line up and spends minutes. That is the exception. The default is to release.
