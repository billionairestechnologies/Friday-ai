# Discussion

The call is a conversation with Friday. It is not a menu, and it is not a script that interviews the user.

## Path

1. The user speaks.
2. Sarvam transcribes the turn and sends it to the Friday session.
3. Friday answers in one turn.
4. That answer is spoken.
5. Friday stops. The line stays quiet until the user speaks again.

If Friday did not understand the job, she asks one question and waits. She does not stack questions. The rest of the spoken shape, including the one curious beat, is [the persona](../models/07-persona.md).

If the user talks over her, barge-in stops her audio and the new turn replaces the rest of that reply.

## What she does not do

- Talk again without a new user turn
- Fill silence with status chatter on a held short job beyond the single "getting the details" line
- Read a questionnaire before she will start

A short job may include that one status line, then the result, as part of the same answer. A long job's answer is the acknowledgement: what she is starting, and where it will show up. Then she waits, or the call ends because the job was released.
